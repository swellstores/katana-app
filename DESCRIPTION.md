Send your Swell orders to Katana as sales orders, and keep order status, tracking and stock in sync. When a sales order is delivered in Katana, the Swell order is fulfilled with the carrier and tracking number, and Swell stock follows Katana's stock at your location.

Connect Katana with an API key and the ID of the location that ships your orders. From then on, paid orders go to Katana as they come in, and the app checks Katana for changes every 5 minutes.

- **Orders become sales orders.** Paid orders, or submitted ones if you choose, go to Katana with the customer, addresses, items matched by SKU, discounts and the shipping fee. Canceled orders are removed from Katana while they haven't been packed or delivered.
- **Fulfilled from Katana.** When a sales order is delivered in Katana, the Swell order gets a shipment with the carrier and tracking number. A Katana tab on each order shows the sales order, status, tracking and any error.
- **Stock that follows Katana.** Swell stock is set from Katana's stock at your location, every 5 minutes for items that moved and for every item once an hour. Each change shows in the product's stock history.
- **Your products in Katana.** Create your Swell products in Katana with their variants, prices and costs, and copy your Swell stock as Katana's starting stock.
- **Big syncs as tasks.** Sync all stock from Katana, send orders placed before you installed the app, or create products in Katana, each as a task with progress, cancel and resume.
- **Errors where you look for them.** Orders with a missing or duplicate SKU aren't sent, and the order's Katana tab lists the SKUs to fix. Every check that changed something is logged under Katana pulls.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Katana, create an API key under Settings → API. In Swell, enter it in the app settings, run Test connection to find your location ID, and save it. Make sure your Swell products and variants have the same SKUs as in Katana.
