Send your Swell orders to Katana Cloud Inventory as sales orders, and keep order status, tracking and stock in sync. Every 5 minutes the app brings changes from Katana back to Swell: when a sales order is delivered, the Swell order is fulfilled with its tracking number, and Swell stock follows Katana inventory at your location.

- **Orders become sales orders.** Paid orders, or submitted ones if you choose, are created in Katana with the customer, addresses, items matched by SKU, discounts and shipping. Canceled orders are removed from Katana while they haven't shipped.
- **Fulfilled from Katana.** When a sales order is delivered in Katana, the Swell order gets a shipment with the carrier and tracking number. A Katana tab on each order shows the sales order, status, tracking and any error.
- **Stock that follows Katana.** Swell stock is set from Katana inventory at your selling location, every 5 minutes for items that moved and with an hourly full sync. Every change is noted in the product's stock history.
- **Big syncs in one click.** Sync all stock from Katana, send orders placed before you installed the app, or create your Swell products in Katana with their starting stock. Each runs as a task with live progress under Katana tasks.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Katana, create an API key in Settings → API. In Swell, enter it in the app settings with the ID of the Katana location that ships your orders, and run Test connection. Make sure your Swell products and variants have the same SKUs as in Katana.
